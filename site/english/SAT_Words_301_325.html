301

Substantiate

verb
Meaning

To support or prove a claim with evidence or facts.

Synonyms

support, verify, corroborate

Antonyms

refute, disprove

Example sentence

“The researcher used additional data to substantiate her conclusion.”

302

Caveat

noun
Meaning

A warning, condition, or limitation that must be considered.

Synonyms

warning, qualification, proviso

Antonyms

assurance, guarantee

Example sentence

“The recommendation came with the caveat that the study involved only a small sample.”

303

Corollary

noun
Meaning

A result or conclusion that follows naturally from an established fact or principle.

Synonyms

consequence, result, deduction

Antonyms

premise, cause

Example sentence

“A corollary of longer life expectancy is a growing need for elder care.”

304

Dichotomy

noun
Meaning

A division or contrast between two sharply different ideas or groups.

Synonyms

division, contrast, split

Antonyms

unity, continuity

Example sentence

“The essay challenges the false dichotomy between economic growth and environmental protection.”

305

Ramification

noun
Meaning

A consequence, especially one that is indirect or complicated.

Synonyms

consequence, implication, repercussion

Antonyms

cause, origin

Example sentence

“The committee considered every ramification of changing the admission policy.”

Synonyms

Antonyms

central, relevant

Example sentence

“The speaker omitted a tangential anecdote so the presentation would remain focused.”

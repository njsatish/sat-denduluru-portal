Concept guide

Ratios, Rates, Proportions, and Units

Solve multi-step rate, scale, density, and conversion problems.

  • Build unit rates by division.
  • Scale proportional quantities with one multiplier.
  • Arrange conversion factors so units cancel.
rate=quantity/timedensity=mass/volume
Common mistake: Mixing units before conversion produces invalid rates.
Concept guide

Percentages and Multi-Step Percent Change

Apply, combine, and reverse percentage multipliers.

  • Discount uses 1−r.
  • Tax or markup uses 1+r.
  • Successive percent changes multiply.
new=original(1±r)original=final/multiplier
Common mistake: An increase and equal decrease do not cancel.
Concept guide

Statistics and Data Distributions

Reason about totals, transformations, center, and spread.

  • Mean × count reconstructs the total.
  • Adding a constant shifts the mean only.
  • Multiplying values scales mean and standard deviation.
mean=sum/countSD(ax+b)=|a|SD(x)
Common mistake: Do not average averages without considering group sizes.
Concept guide

Scatterplots and Mathematical Models

Interpret slope, predictions, residuals, and model limits.

  • Residual is observed minus predicted.
  • Slope gives predicted change per unit x.
  • Avoid extrapolation beyond the model's stated range.
ŷ=mx+bresidual=y−ŷ
Common mistake: Reversing residual subtraction changes its sign.
Concept guide

Probability, Counting, and Sampling

Handle dependence, arrangements, combinations, and inference.

  • Without replacement changes probabilities.
  • Distinct roles mean order matters.
  • Representative random samples support population estimates.
P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B|A)P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B)
Common mistake: Do not treat dependent events as independent.
Concept guide

Area, Surface Area, Volume, and Similarity

Combine solids and apply linear, area, and volume scale factors.

  • Area scales by k².
  • Volume scales by k³.
  • Composite measurements use addition or subtraction.
Vcylinder=πr²hVcone=(1/3)πr²h
Common mistake: A radius scale factor does not apply directly to volume.
Concept guide

Lines, Angles, and Triangles

Use angle algebra, parallel-line relationships, and similarity.

  • Triangle angles sum to 180°.
  • Same-side interior angles are supplementary.
  • Corresponding sides of similar triangles share one scale factor.
exterior angle=sum of remote interiors
Common mistake: Match corresponding sides before forming proportions.
Concept guide

Coordinate Geometry

Use midpoint, distance, slope, perpendicularity, and polygon area.

  • Midpoint averages coordinates.
  • Distance comes from the Pythagorean theorem.
  • Perpendicular slopes are opposite reciprocals.
d=√((x₂−x₁)²+(y₂−y₁)²)m₁m₂=−1
Common mistake: Vertical lines require special care because slope is undefined.
Concept guide

Right Triangles and Trigonometry

Connect side ratios, special triangles, and contextual lengths.

  • SOH-CAH-TOA links angles and sides.
  • 30-60-90 sides follow 1:√3:2.
  • 45-45-90 sides follow 1:1:√2.
a²+b²=c²sin=opp/hyp
Common mistake: Identify the hypotenuse before choosing a trig ratio.
Concept guide

Circles

Work with arcs, sectors, equations, and tangency.

  • Arc length uses the angle fraction of circumference.
  • Sector area uses the angle fraction of area.
  • Tangency radius is perpendicular to the tangent line.
L=(θ/360)2πrAsector=(θ/360)πr²
Common mistake: In a circle equation, the right side is r², not r.

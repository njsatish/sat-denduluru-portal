Concept guide

Equivalent Expressions and Factoring

Rewrite expressions without changing their value.

  • Distribute before combining like terms.
  • Use patterns such as difference of squares.
  • Track exponent rules carefully.
a(b + c) = ab + acA² − B² = (A − B)(A + B)xᵐxⁿ = xᵐ⁺ⁿ
Common mistake: Only like terms—same variables with the same exponents—can be combined.
Concept guide

Quadratic Equations and Functions

Use roots, factors, vertices, and graphs to analyze quadratics.

  • Factoring reveals zeros.
  • Vertex form reveals maximum or minimum.
  • The discriminant tells how many real solutions exist.
y = ax² + bx + cy = a(x − h)² + kb² − 4ac
Common mistake: A negative discriminant means no real solutions, not no solutions at all.

Exponential Functions

Recognize repeated multiplication, growth, and decay.

  • Growth factor = 1 + r.
  • Decay factor = 1 − r.
  • The exponent counts equal time periods.
y = a(b)ˣA = P(1 + r)ᵗ
Common mistake: A 20% increase uses 1.20, not 0.20.

Polynomial and Rational Expressions

Operate on polynomials and rational expressions while respecting structure and domain.

  • Combine matching powers.
  • Use the Remainder Theorem for division by x − k.
  • Exclude values that make a denominator zero.
P(k) = remainderxᵐ/xⁿ = xᵐ⁻ⁿ
Common mistake: Cancellation does not erase original domain restrictions.
Concept guide

Radical and Rational Equations

Solve equations containing roots, fractions, and inverse variation.

  • Square both sides carefully.
  • Check possible extraneous solutions.
  • Multiply by a common denominator to clear fractions.
√A = b ⇒ A = b²xy = k
Common mistake: Squaring can introduce a solution that fails the original equation.
Concept guide

Nonlinear Functions and Systems

Analyze compositions, inverses, transformations, absolute value, and nonlinear intersections.

  • Evaluate inner functions first.
  • An inverse reverses input and output.
  • Absolute-value equations often produce two cases.
(f ∘ g)(x) = f(g(x))|x − h| = k ⇒ x = h ± k
Common mistake: Horizontal shifts use the opposite sign inside the function.
